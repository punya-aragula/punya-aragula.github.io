A bit about me

I'm a second-year PhD student in Information Science at the School of Data and Information Sciences, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, advised by Dr. Marijel (Maggie) Melo. I'm also a graduate researcher at the Society-Centered AI Lab (SAIL) with Dr. Neil Gaikwad.

I look at how people come to trust, rely on, and make sense of AI in their own social and cultural contexts, with a focus on communities in the Global South and others rarely consulted in how AI is built and governed. My work combines cross-cultural qualitative and participatory research with conceptual work grounded in the capability approach, bounded rationality, and care ethics.

I took an interdisciplinary and winding route to get here, and I think it shapes how I see people and technology. Follow the path ↓
