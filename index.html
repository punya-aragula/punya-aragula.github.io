A bit about me

I'm a PhD student in Information Science at the School of Data and Information Science, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, advised by Dr. Marijel Melo. I'm also a graduate researcher at the Society-Centered AI Lab (SAIL) with Dr. Neil Gaikwad.

I look at how people come to trust, rely on, and make sense of AI in their own social and cultural contexts, with a focus on communities in the Global South and others rarely consulted in how AI is built and governed. My work combines cross-cultural qualitative and participatory research with conceptual work grounded in the capability approach, bounded rationality, and care ethics.

My path here has been a winding one: a B.Sc. in Biology at Azim Premji University, an M.Sc. in Neuropsychology at National Forensic Sciences University, and an M.S. in Human–Computer Interaction at Iowa State University.
