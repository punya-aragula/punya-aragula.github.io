I study how people live with AI, and what their experiences should teach us about how AI is evaluated and governed.

I’m a second-year PhD student in Information Science at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. I’m advised by Dr. Marijel (Maggie) Melo, my primary academic advisor, and by Dr. Neil Gaikwad, my research advisor for projects in the Society-Centered AI Lab (SAIL), where I’m a graduate researcher.

I look at how people come to trust, rely on, and make sense of AI in their own social and cultural contexts, with a focus on communities in the Global South and others rarely consulted in how AI is built and governed. My work combines cross-cultural qualitative and participatory research, and draws on care ethics and Global South philosophies.

I took an interdisciplinary and winding route to get here, and I think it shapes how I see people and technology. Follow the path ↓
